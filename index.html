Why Stashfin

We are not selling you the same thing twice

Most appliance brands plan for you to come back in eighteen months. We would rather you came back for a spare gasket.

01

Motors that hold up

Every motorised unit is rated at its true continuous wattage, not a peak figure pulled for the box. If it says 750W, it delivers 750W under load.

02

Parts you can replace

Jars, gaskets, crumb trays and blades all ship as individual spares. A worn component should cost you a part, not a whole new appliance.

03

A warranty we honour

Two years standard across the line, handled directly by our own team. No third-party claim portals and no runaround on service requests.
